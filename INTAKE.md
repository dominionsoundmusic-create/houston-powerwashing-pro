WHAT THE BUSINESS ACTUALLY IS (read this twice):
Houston Power Washing Pro is a free phone line and website, NOT a pressure washing company. It does not own a pressure washer and never cleans anything itself. When someone calls (832) 662-4107:
- An automated assistant answers, any hour. The same number also takes roofing and air conditioning calls, so it first asks which one the caller needs.
- The caller says what needs cleaning and roughly how big it is.
- The call is transferred to an independent, owner-run local pressure washing company, which hears a short summary before it picks up.
- If that company is out on a job, the caller's name, number and need are passed along for a call back.
The company the caller is connected with inspects, quotes, does the work and is paid directly by the customer. Houston Power Washing Pro does not quote, schedule, supervise or guarantee the work, and never charges the customer. It may be paid a referral fee by the companies it connects callers with (this is already disclosed on the current About page; keep disclosing it).
Never name the partner company on the site. Never say "we clean", "our crew", "our technicians", "we use eco-friendly products", or anything that implies Houston Power Washing Pro does the work.
