Gutters are the reason the wall underneath is stained

Most people book gutter cleaning because the gutters are overflowing. The more useful reason is what the overflow does to everything below it.

A blocked gutter does not simply fail to drain. It sends water over the front lip in a sheet, straight down the fascia and the wall beneath. That is where the vertical dark streaks come from — and cleaning the wall without clearing the gutter means the streak returns with the next storm.

What blocks them here

  • Pine needles are the worst offenders. They mat together into a felt that water cannot pass and that holds moisture permanently.
  • Oak catkins and leaves in spring and autumn, which break down into a sludge at the low end.
  • Shingle granules, which build up as a roof ages. A gutter full of grit is a roof telling you something.
  • Nests, usually at the downspout opening, which is the narrowest point.

If you are finding a lot of shingle granules in the gutter and the roof is more than fifteen years old, mention it. It does not mean the roof is failing, but it is worth knowing about rather than washing away without noticing.

Cleaning out is not the same as cleaning up

Two different jobs get called gutter cleaning, and it is worth knowing which you are buying.

JobWhat it means
ClearingRemoving debris from inside the gutter and flushing the downspouts so water runs. This is the functional job
BrighteningCleaning the outside face of the gutter, where oxidation and those vertical tiger stripes build up. This is cosmetic and it is a separate process with a separate product

Those grey vertical stripes on white gutters do not come off with pressure or ordinary soap. They are oxidised aluminium plus road film and they need a specific cleaner. Ask which you are getting.

Check the downspouts, not just the gutters

A gutter can be spotless and still overflow if the downspout is blocked at the elbow or the underground run is full. The test is simple: water poured in at the far end should come out at the bottom within seconds. If it backs up, the blockage is below the gutter and clearing the trough achieved nothing.

How often

Twice a year is the usual advice. Under heavy pine or oak canopy, three times is more realistic, with one of those after the main needle drop. Houses with no overhanging trees can often go a year.

Gutter guards reduce the frequency rather than eliminating it. Fine debris still gets through and sits under the guard, where it is harder to reach.

Worth bundling

If you are having the roof or the house cleaned, gutters are usually offered as an add-on, and the reason is practical: the crew is already set up at height. Doing it separately means paying twice for the same access.
