If you got a letter from your HOA

Cypress is largely master-planned — Bridgeland, Fairfield, Towne Lake, Coles Crossing, Blackhorse Ranch and the rest — which means most homes here sit under deed restrictions with an association that inspects. Driveways, siding and fences are the three things those inspections flag most often.

Two things worth knowing before you panic about the letter.

  • Read the deadline, not the tone. Most associations give thirty days from the date on the letter. The language is usually firmer than the timeline.
  • Tell them work is scheduled. A reply saying the cleaning is booked for a specific date will usually stop the clock. Associations are trying to get the problem fixed, not collect fines.

If your deadline is close, say so when you call. A driveway and walkway is often a half-day job, and getting it done this week is usually possible. What you do not want is to book the cheapest quote three weeks out and miss the date.

Why Cypress houses go green on one side

Homeowners here often notice that one wall looks years older than the other three. That is not paint failure. North-facing walls get the least direct sun, so they stay damp longest, and Gulf Coast humidity does the rest. Algae takes hold where moisture lingers.

The same logic explains the shaded stretch of driveway under an oak, the north side of a fence, and the roof slope that faces away from the afternoon sun. It is a moisture problem, not a dirt problem, which is why rinsing it with a garden hose does nothing and it comes back within weeks of a bad clean.

Pressure washing and soft washing are not the same job

This is the single most useful thing to understand before you hire anyone, because it tells you whether the person quoting knows what they are doing.

SurfaceWhat it needs
Concrete driveway, walkway, patioPressure. Concrete can take it, and it is what lifts embedded dirt and tire marks
Siding, stucco, brickSoft wash. Low pressure plus a cleaning solution that kills the algae rather than blasting it off
Roof shinglesSoft wash only. Pressure strips the granules and shortens the roof's life
Wood fence or deckLow pressure. Too much and you raise the grain, leaving a fuzzy surface that greys again fast

If someone offers to pressure wash your siding, that is a useful signal. High pressure on siding drives water behind the panels, where it has no easy way out. On stucco it can take the finish off entirely.

What to ask before you book

  • Are you soft washing the house or pressure washing it? The answer should be soft washing, and they should not need to think about it.
  • What are you putting on my plants? Soft wash solutions can damage landscaping if beds are not wetted down first and rinsed after. Ask how they handle it.
  • Is the quote for the driveway only, or the walkway and entry too? This is where cheap quotes get their price. The apron, the path to the front door and the porch are often extra.
  • Are you insured? Water forced into the wrong place is an expensive mistake on a two-storey house.
  • How long until it comes back? Anyone who says never is guessing. In this climate, a good soft wash on siding typically holds for a year or two depending on shade and drainage.

When to have it done

Spring and early autumn are the comfortable windows. Deep summer works but the surfaces dry fast, which makes some cleaning solutions less effective and means a good crew will work in smaller sections. Winter is fine here most years — the limiting factor is a hard freeze, which Cypress gets rarely and briefly.

If you are selling, do it before the photographs rather than before the showing. A clean driveway and a clean north wall change how a house reads in a listing photo more than almost anything else you can do for the money.
