How Often Should You Pressure Wash Your House? A Houston Homeowner's Guide

If you've lived in Houston for more than a year, you already know the struggle. The heat, the humidity, the afternoon thunderstorms, and the relentless pollen season all combine to turn your home's exterior into a magnet for dirt, mold, mildew, and algae. But how often should you actually pressure wash your house to keep it looking great and protect your investment? The answer depends on a few key factors — and we're going to walk you through all of them.

Whether you own a brick ranch home in Katy, a stucco beauty in Sugar Land, or a classic craftsman in the Heights, this guide will help you figure out the right pressure washing schedule for your specific situation.

The General Rule: Once a Year Is the Minimum

For most Houston homeowners, pressure washing your house at least once per year is the baseline recommendation. An annual cleaning helps remove the buildup of dirt, pollen, mold spores, and biological growth that accumulates on siding, brick, stucco, and other exterior surfaces over time.

Spring is typically the best time to schedule your annual pressure wash. After pollen season peaks in Houston — usually between February and April — a thorough exterior cleaning removes that thick yellow-green coating from every surface of your home. It also prepares your property for the summer months when heat and humidity accelerate mold and mildew growth.

That said, once a year is just the starting point. Depending on where your home sits, what it's surrounded by, and what materials it's made from, you may need professional power washing services more frequently.

Signs Your Houston Home Needs Pressure Washing Right Now

Don't wait for your annual cleaning if you're noticing any of these warning signs on your home's exterior:

  • Green or black streaks on siding or brick — This is algae, mold, or mildew. In Houston's humid climate, these organisms spread quickly and can cause long-term damage if left untreated.
  • Visible dirt and grime buildup — If your home's original color looks dull or discolored, it's time for a wash.
  • Cobwebs and insect nests around eaves and corners — These accumulate faster than most homeowners realize.
  • Staining near the roofline — Dark streaks running down from your roof often indicate algae growth that started on your shingles.
  • Slippery walkways, driveways, or decks — Slimy buildup on concrete and wood surfaces is a safety hazard and a clear sign it's past time to clean.
  • Preparing to paint or re-seal — Any time you plan to repaint your home's exterior or apply a fresh sealant, a professional pressure wash first is absolutely essential for proper adhesion.

If you're seeing two or more of these signs, don't wait for your scheduled cleaning date. Addressing mold and mildew early saves you money on repairs down the road.

How Houston's Climate Affects Your Pressure Washing Schedule

Houston is not an average city when it comes to weather — and that directly impacts how often your home needs to be cleaned. Here's what makes the Houston area uniquely challenging for homeowners:

High humidity year-round: With average humidity levels often exceeding 70%, Houston creates the perfect breeding ground for mold, mildew, and algae on exterior surfaces. Homes in shaded areas or near bayous and waterways are especially vulnerable.

Intense pollen seasons: Houston consistently ranks among the worst cities in the country for allergies. The pollen that coats your car also coats your home — and it doesn't just look bad. Pollen buildup can trap moisture against your siding, accelerating biological growth.

Hurricane season and heavy rains: While rain rinses some dirt away, heavy storms also deposit debris, mud, and contaminants onto your home's exterior. After a major storm system moves through, a follow-up pressure wash is often a smart move.

Tree coverage: Houston's beautiful live oaks and other mature trees drop sap, leaves, and organic debris that stain and damage exterior surfaces. Homes surrounded by heavy tree canopy often need cleaning two to three times per year.

Bottom line: Houston homeowners typically need to pressure wash more often than homeowners in drier, cooler climates. Twice a year is a reasonable schedule for most Houston properties.

Pressure Washing Frequency by Surface Type

Different surfaces on your property have different cleaning needs. Here's a quick breakdown:

  • Vinyl and aluminum siding: Once a year is typically sufficient, though twice a year is better in humid or heavily wooded areas.
  • Brick exteriors: Once every one to two years. Brick is durable but the mortar and surface can trap dirt and biological growth over time.
  • Stucco: Once a year minimum. Stucco's textured surface traps dirt and moisture and can develop mold quickly in Houston's climate.
  • Concrete driveways and walkways: Once or twice a year depending on traffic and oil staining. Houston's heat causes concrete to absorb stains faster.
  • Wood decks and fences: Once a year before re-sealing or staining. Neglected wood surfaces in Houston can develop mold and rot surprisingly fast.
  • Roof (soft wash): Every one to three years using a low-pressure soft wash method to safely remove algae and moss without damaging shingles.

The Real Cost of Waiting Too Long

Some homeowners put off pressure washing because it seems like a cosmetic concern. In reality, skipping regular exterior cleanings can lead to serious and expensive problems:

Mold and mildew don't just sit on the surface — over time, they penetrate siding, wood, and grout, causing structural deterioration. Algae growth on a roof can shorten its lifespan by years. Stained and grimy concrete begins to crack and erode more quickly. And a neglected exterior dramatically reduces your home's curb appeal, which matters whether you're planning to sell or simply take pride in your property.

Investing in routine pressure washing every one to two years is far less expensive than replacing siding, repairing wood rot, or repainting a home that wasn't properly maintained. Think of it like regular oil changes for your car — a small, consistent investment that prevents major breakdowns.

DIY vs. Professional Pressure Washing: What Houston Homeowners Should Know

Renting a pressure washer from a hardware store might seem like a cost-effective option, but there are real risks to going the DIY route. Too much pressure can strip paint, damage wood, force water behind siding, and even crack mortar in brick. Soft wash techniques — which use lower pressure combined with professional-grade cleaning solutions — require expertise and the right equipment to be done safely and effectively.

A professional power washing company in Houston will also know how to handle the specific types of staining, biological growth, and surface materials common in this area. They carry the right insurance, use biodegradable cleaning agents, and can complete the job efficiently and safely.

For most Houston homeowners, the combination of professional results, time savings, and protection against accidental damage makes hiring a local power washing pro the smarter choice.
