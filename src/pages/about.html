What Houston Power Washing Pro is

Houston Power Washing Pro is a phone line and this website. It does not own a pressure washer, employ cleaners or visit homes. Its one job is to answer your call and put you through to an independent, owner-run pressure washing company in the Houston area, which will look at what needs cleaning, give you a price and do the work if you hire it.

That company is the one you hire, the one you pay and the one responsible for the job. Houston Power Washing Pro does not quote, schedule, supervise or guarantee the work.

  • AreaHouston and surrounding communities

Why it exists

Getting the outside of a house cleaned in Houston usually starts with a search, a list of companies and a string of voicemails. The line shortens that to one call that is answered at any hour and handed straight to a local business that does the work. It also explains, page by page, what good pressure washing and soft washing look like, so you can judge whoever you hire.

How it is paid

You never pay the line. Houston Power Washing Pro may be paid a referral fee by the companies it connects callers with. You are free to get other quotes and to hire anyone you like.

What you will not find here

You will not see star ratings, review counts, "years in business," job totals or guarantees on this site. Houston Power Washing Pro does not do the work, so it has none of those to show, and it will not borrow or invent them. Ask the company you are connected with for its own references, insurance and written terms.

What to ask any company

Pressure washing is not one of the trades licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, so these questions stand in for a license check:

  • Can you show proof of liability insurance?
  • Will you write down the price for each surface?
  • Will you soft wash the siding, roof and any wood rather than blast them?
