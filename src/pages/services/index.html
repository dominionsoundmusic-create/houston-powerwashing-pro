Every service below is quoted and carried out by an independent, owner-run local pressure washing company. Houston Power Washing Pro answers the phone and puts you through. Use this page to work out which service fits what you are looking at, so the call is quick.

The biggest difference between services is the method. Hard surfaces like concrete get pressure washing: high-pressure water, usually through a rotary surface cleaner. Siding, roofs and most wood get soft washing: low pressure with a cleaning solution that kills mildew and algae so a gentle rinse carries it away. A careful company switches between the two on the same property.
