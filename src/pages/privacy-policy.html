Houston Power Washing Pro is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group ("Houston Power Washing Pro," "the line," "we," "us"). It operates houstonpowerwashingpro.com and the phone line (832) 662-4107. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the website or the phone line, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

Houston Power Washing Pro is a referral phone line, not a pressure washing company. It connects you with an independent local company, which quotes and does any work. That company has its own privacy practices.

Information collected

  • Information you give. When you call, the line collects your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, city or ZIP code, and the details you describe about the work you need.
  • Call records. When you call, the line keeps your phone number and a record of the call, which may include a summary of what you said to the automated assistant, so your request can be passed along and followed up.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the site and its hosting provider log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring site. This is used to keep the site running and to understand how it is used.

How information is used

  • To pass your request to the independent local company handling it, so it can contact you
  • To return a call or follow up on a request you made
  • To operate, secure and improve the website and phone line
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

Text messages

If you give a mobile number and agree to receive text messages, you may receive messages about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for help. Agreeing to text messages is not a condition of any purchase. Mobile numbers and text message consent are not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing.

How information is shared

Your personal information is not sold. When you call, your name, phone number, location and the details of the job are shared with the independent local company you are connected with, so that it can contact you and quote the work. Otherwise, information is shared only with service providers that help run the line and website (such as phone, form, hosting and translation providers), and only as far as they need it to do that work. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect legal rights.

Cookies, translation and analytics

The site uses a small number of cookies to work properly. If you choose Español in the header, a Google Translate cookie remembers that choice and Google's translation service processes the page text. If analytics are added to the site, this policy will be updated to name them. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies; doing so may affect how parts of the site work.

Data retention and security

Records are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect the information held, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to see the personal information held about you, correct it, or delete it, subject to records that must be kept by law. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. To make a request, call (832) 662-4107.

Children

The site is meant for adults arranging work on a property. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call (832) 662-4107 any hour. See also the Terms of Use.

This policy describes current practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by the business owner before launch.
