What happens when you call

Tips for a useful request

  • List every surface you want done: the company can plan one visit instead of two.
  • Give a rough size, like "two-car driveway" or "two-story house."
  • Mention any deadline, such as an HOA letter date or a closing.
  • Note anything delicate: fresh paint, new landscaping, a koi pond, an outdoor kitchen.
