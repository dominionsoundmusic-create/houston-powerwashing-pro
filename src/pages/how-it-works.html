Houston Power Washing Pro exists to make one step easier: getting a local pressure washing company on the phone. It does not clean anything itself. Here is what happens from the moment you dial (832) 662-4107.

Step by step

  1. An automated assistant answers, day or night. The line is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The same number also handles roofing and air conditioning, so the assistant first asks which one you need. Say pressure washing.
  2. You describe what needs cleaning. The assistant asks what needs cleaning and roughly how big it is. Plain answers are fine: "the driveway and the front of the house," "a two-story house with green on the back," "the storefront sidewalk."
  3. You are transferred to a local company. Your call goes to an independent, owner-run pressure washing company in the Houston area. It hears a short summary of what you said before it picks up, so you do not start from scratch.
  4. If the company is out on a job, it calls you back. Your name, number and what you need are passed along so the company can return your call.
  5. You work things out directly with that company. It may look at photos or come to the property, then give you its own price. If you agree, it schedules and does the work, and you pay it directly.

What the line does

  • Answers any hour, every day
  • Takes down what needs cleaning, how big it is and where
  • Puts you through to an independent local pressure washing company
  • Passes your details along for a call back if the company is busy
  • Costs you nothing

What the line does not do

  • Clean anything or send anyone to your home
  • Give you a price or a quote
  • Schedule, supervise or inspect the work
  • Guarantee the work, the timing or the price
  • Charge you, ever

What to have ready before you call

None of this is required, but it helps the company give you a useful answer faster.

  • The surfacesDriveway, walks, house, roof, fence, deck, gutters, storefront.
  • Rough size"Two-car driveway," "two-story, about 2,500 square feet," "100 feet of fence."
  • What you seeGreen mildew, black roof streaks, oil spots, orange rust rings, gray wood.
  • Any deadlineAn HOA letter date, a closing, a party, a stain you plan to apply.

Photos are useful too. The company may ask you to text a few once it is talking to you.

How the line is paid

Houston Power Washing Pro is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group. It never charges callers. It may be paid a referral fee by the companies it connects callers with. That fee does not come from you and does not change who you are allowed to hire. You are free to get other quotes, and you should choose whichever company you are comfortable with.

Hiring the company well

Because the line does not inspect or guarantee anyone's work, ask the company you talk to the same questions you would ask any contractor: proof of liability insurance, a written price for each surface, and whether siding, roofs and wood will be soft washed rather than blasted. The pressure washing quote guide lists more.
