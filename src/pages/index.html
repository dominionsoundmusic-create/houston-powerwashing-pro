Why Houston homes need cleaning more often

Mildew and algae grow best in warm, damp air, and Houston mornings often start near 90 percent humidity. The north side of a house, a walk beside a flower bed and a roof under trees can stay damp for days after a storm.

Each season adds something. Oak pollen coats everything yellow in early spring. Summer thunderstorms splash soil onto slabs. Hurricane season, June through November, leaves silt and leaf stains behind. That is why so many Houston-area HOAs write letters about dark driveways and green siding.

Before you hire any pressure washing company

Texas has no state license for this trade (the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation does not cover pressure washing), so a license lookup will not tell you much. Three questions will:

  • Proof of liability insurance. A pressure washer can break a window or strip paint. Make sure the company can pay for a mistake.
  • A written price for each surface. Driveway, house, roof and fence listed separately makes two quotes easy to compare.
  • Soft washing for siding, roofs and wood. High pressure on these surfaces can force water behind siding, strip roof granules and fuzz wood. Ask how they will clean each one.
