Common questions

Is Houston Power Washing Pro a cleaning company?

No. It is a phone line and website that connects you with independent local pressure washing companies. They quote and do the cleaning, and you pay them directly.

Who answers when I call?

An automated assistant answers around the clock, asks what needs cleaning and where, and transfers you to a local company. If they are on a job, your details are passed along.

Does it cost anything to call?

No. Houston Power Washing Pro may be paid a referral fee by the companies it connects callers with, but you never pay the line.

What is soft washing?

Cleaning with a solution and low pressure instead of high pressure. It is the right method for roofs, painted siding, stucco and most wood, which high pressure can damage.

How is pressure washing priced?

By surface, size and condition. A driveway, a house and a roof are each priced differently. The cost guide gives typical ranges.

Are pressure washing companies licensed in Texas?

No state license exists. Ask for proof of liability insurance and a written price.

Will the chemicals hurt my plants or the drains?

A careful company wets and rinses plants, uses only as much solution as needed and keeps heavily soiled water out of storm drains. Some cities, such as Sugar Land, prohibit dumping wash water into the storm system.

How often should a house be washed?

Every year or two for most Houston homes, more often under heavy trees or near the coast.

What areas do you cover?

The Houston area, including The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Katy, Cypress, Spring, Pearland, Conroe, League City, Friendswood, Kingwood and Missouri City.
