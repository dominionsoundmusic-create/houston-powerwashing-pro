In Sugar Land, the wash water rule has teeth

Sugar Land is built around water. First Colony, Telfair, Sugar Creek and many other neighborhoods wrap around lakes and detention ponds, and the storm drains in your street feed them directly. That is why the city takes runoff seriously. Sugar Land's Stormwater Ordinance No. 1788, passed in 2010, prohibits illicit discharges into the storm drain system, and the city says any homeowner or business found discharging waste or wastewater into it may face legal penalties.

Plain rinse water is not the concern. Cleaning solutions, oil from a stained driveway and the grime lifted off a house are. A careful pressure washing company works with that in mind: it pre-wets and rinses plants, measures cleaning solution rather than flooding the surface, and keeps heavily soiled water from running down the curb.

Ask before the truck arrives: "How do you keep wash water and chemicals out of the storm drain?" In Sugar Land, the property owner and the contractor both have a stake in the answer.

What Sugar Land homes need cleaned

The typical Sugar Land house is brick on the lower walls with painted siding or trim above, a two-car driveway and a wood fence. Each wants a different approach:

  • Brick: low to moderate pressure, and care around mortar joints on older houses.
  • Painted siding and trim: soft wash only; high pressure lifts paint and forces water behind boards.
  • Concrete driveways: surface cleaner and pressure, with oil spots pre-treated.
  • Fences: low pressure with a cleaner, so the wood is cleaned, not shredded.

Common questions

Is it illegal to pressure wash into the storm drain in Sugar Land?

Sugar Land's Stormwater Ordinance No. 1788 prohibits illicit discharges into the storm drain system, and the city says discharging waste or wastewater can bring penalties. Keep cleaners, oil and heavy grime out of the drains.

Can brick be pressure washed?

Yes, at low to moderate pressure. Older mortar can be damaged by high pressure, so ask how the company will clean it.

Should painted siding be pressure washed?

No. It should be soft washed with a cleaning solution and low pressure to avoid lifting paint and forcing water behind the siding.

Are pressure washing companies licensed in Texas?

Texas has no state license for pressure washing. Ask for proof of liability insurance and a written price.
