What Houston does to a wood fence

Most fences in Greater Houston are pine or cedar pickets, often board-on-board, and the Gulf climate works on them from every direction. Humidity keeps the shaded side damp, so green and black mildew grows almost year-round. Sun grays the exposed side. Sprinklers and splashback stain the bottom boards. And the posts sit in wet clay, which is where rot and termites start.

How a fence should be cleaned

  • Low pressure and a wide tip, moved along the grain. High pressure tears the soft wood between the grain lines and leaves the fence fuzzy.
  • A wood cleaner first, to loosen mildew and gray so the rinse does not have to scrape.
  • A brightener after, which restores the color and gets the wood ready for stain.
  • Plants and grass along the fence line wetted first and rinsed after.

Shared fence? Your neighbor's side is their call. Agree before the crew starts, and it is often worth splitting the job so both sides match.

Staining in Houston humidity

Cleaning is the first half of the job. If the fence is going to be stained or sealed, the wood has to dry properly first, and in Houston that can take longer than the label on the can suggests. Wait for a run of dry days after cleaning. Stain over damp wood traps moisture and peels. A penetrating stain with mildewcide suits the climate better than a film-forming one.

Look at the posts while it is clean

Check each post at ground level for soft wood, and look for mud tubes on posts and boards near the ground. Houston has active termite populations, including Formosan termites, and a fence is often where they are first noticed. Leaning sections and loose pickets are cheaper to fix before the next storm takes them down.

Common questions

How often should a fence be cleaned in Houston?

Every year or two for most fences, more often for the shaded side, which grows mildew quickly in Gulf Coast humidity.

Will cleaning damage my fence?

Not when it is done at low pressure with a wood cleaner. High pressure held close can tear the wood fibers and leave the surface fuzzy.

How long after cleaning can I stain?

Once the wood has dried thoroughly, which in Houston often means waiting for several dry days after cleaning.

Can a gray fence look new again?

Often close to it. A cleaner and brightener remove most of the weathered gray and restore much of the wood's color.
