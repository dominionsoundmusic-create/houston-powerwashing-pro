# Prompts 18 and 19: Responsive, accessibility and functional QA

Tool: `scripts/qa.js` (Playwright, real Chromium) against the built `dist/` served locally.
Final run: **38 routes x 5 widths (1440, 1280, 768, 414, 360) + interaction checks: 0 failures.**

## Per route, at every width (all 38 routes passed)
- HTTP 200 on direct load
- Exactly one visible H1
- Hero image loaded on first paint and not lazy; desktop heroes on full-hero pages at least 600px tall
- Lead form present in the first response (all marketing pages and contact)
- Every image loads after scrolling (no broken images)
- No horizontal overflow; no heading, button, table, form or image pushed outside the viewport
- Mobile (414, 360): every button, nav link and form field at least 40px tall and wide
- No console or page errors from the site (blocked Google Fonts/Translate requests in this sandbox ignored)

## Interaction checks (passed)
- Mobile menu opens, sets aria-expanded, closes with Escape
- Services submenu opens on tap and its links navigate; destination page starts at the top
- Browser back and forward, and reload (form still present after reload)
- FAQ accordions open and close
- Keyboard: first Tab reaches "Skip to content"; focused elements show a visible outline; Guides menu opens
  with Enter and closes with Escape
- EN/ES toggle sets and clears the Google Translate cookie and marks the active language
- Invalid nested route (/services/not-a-real-service/) returns 404 with the 404 page
- Reduced motion: transitions switched off

## Accessibility notes
- Brand color pairs all pass WCAG AA (lowest 5.26:1, link blue on tint). Hero text sits on a dark fade.
- Logical heading order (check.py warns on skipped levels: none), descriptive link text, alt text on all
  images, labelled form fields, honeypot hidden from assistive tech, breadcrumbs with aria-current.
- Visual review by screenshot at 1440 and 360/390 for home, services, a service, a guide, a city, contact,
  privacy and service areas.
